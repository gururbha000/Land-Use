Land Use

The LAHD Land Use Section serves as the bridge between city planning incentives and long-term community affordability. When developers utilize city or state land use incentives—such as Transit Oriented Communities (TOC) or Density Bonus provisions—LAHD enforces the affordable housing requirements attached to those entitlements. By reviewing replacement obligations and recording binding covenants, LAHD helps safeguard existing residents while growing LA’s supply of income-restricted housing.

Land Use Applications

Application for a RPO Replacement Unit Determination (RUD)

Projects subject to the RPO that do not qualify to utilize the No Net Loss Declaration must complete an application for an RPO Replacement Unit Determination (RUD) with LAHD. Information provided by the applicant and existing tenant(s), as well as information gathered by LAHD, will be used to determine whether any Protected Units and affordable Protected Units exist.

An RPO RUD can take 12 to 16 weeks to process upon receipt of all required documents. Projects that qualify for processing under Executive Directive 1 may be completed within 30 days upon receipt of all required documents.

COVENANT

A Covenant obligates an owner to designate a specified number and type of dwelling units for occupancy by extremely low, very low, low, or moderate income households, usually for a term of 55 or 99 years. Choose this option to apply for a covenant if you receive a land use concession from the City or are otherwise required to provide housing units restricted to Extremely Low, Very Low, Low, or Moderate income households.

The concessions that require affordable housing covenant may include:

  • Density Bonus
  • Transit-Oriented Communities (TOC)
  • Affordable Replacement Requirement (e.g., Resident Protections Ordinance, Housing Crisis Act, Housing Element, etc.)
  • Parking Variances or Reductions
  • Zone Changes
  • Conversion of illegal units to legal units
  • Discretionary determinations by the Department of City Planning or City Council
